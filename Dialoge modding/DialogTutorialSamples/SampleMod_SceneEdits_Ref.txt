This is a reference for the folders in the .zip file, and the sample mods in them!

Here you can find out which scene each sample mod edits, how to trigger the changes ingame, and what effect components they demonstrate.

---

The effect components explained in these files are:

  - TLAnimation: Animations and animation settings.
  - TLAtmosphereAndLighting: Controls environment lighting and VFX settings, color grading, wind speed, and more.
  - TLAttitudeEvent: Controls the poses and animations the player character and the characters standing behind them (although I believe it can be used
                     for other characters too.) Think Tav crossing their arms, looking up confused, shifting their position during dialog, etc.
  - TLCameraDoF: Changes the depth of field effect for a given camera.
  - TLCameraFoV: Controls the field of view of a given camera.
  - TLEmotionEvent: Controls facial expressions.
  - TLMaterial: Allows you to enable temporary material overrides, including changing existing material settings, toggling their visibility, and more.
  - TLPlayEffectEvent: Plays VFX.
  - TLShapeshift: Swaps in CharacterVisuals from other characters.
  - TLShot: Controls camera angles and camera angle switches.
  - TLShowArmor: Toggles visibility of different camp clothes and armor slots.
  - TLShowPeanuts: Shows and hides the characters standing behind the player, i.e. the peanut gallery.
  - TLShowVisual: Toggles visibility of different visual resources.
  - TLShowWeapon: Shows and hides weapons.
  - TLSoundEvent: Plays sound effects.
  - TLSplatter: Controls skin overlays like blood, sweat, bruises, and dirt.
  - TLSwitchLocationEvent: Controls scene setting changes.
  - TLSwitchStageEvent: Lets you reference a "stage" in the Dialog Scene file to reposition all the characters, cameras, lights, etc. at once.
  - TLTransform: Lets you change positions of different characters/cameras/objects over time.
  - TLVoice: Controls settings for voice lines.

The files in the CommonComponents also explain the different elements of the Dialog Timeline and Scene files as a whole.
