***

- A very large portion of time went into making sure all the Vortex metadata was good, so mod names are now all formatted like `mod page name: mod file name`; they also have prefixes and affixes such as \[OPTIONAL] and \[FILE REPLICATION].
- Configs were overhauled again again, with a large focus on keybind cohesion and such. Read more about it on the [Difficulty Immersion Quality Collection - Misc Files](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/9784) changelog.
- This revision wasn't really supposed to be this, but it is this. Many piles of bug fixes, removals, and VERY particular additions; a little bit of shuffling between DIQ and its addons too. I also went about changing many visual overhauls for NPCs, opting for more grounded and BG3 feeling overhauls from PoisonValentine, a valued member of the community. This revision might disappoint some people, it's a rather conservative approach, but I can't provide the best experience if I don't have standards and draw very specific lines. As always DIQ is focused on cleanliness, compatibly, functionality, cohesion, etc., "quality" is in the name and I'll do what I gotta do.
- With Revision 145 of DIQ a higher standard was adopted for head preset inclusions, unfortunately this means many very great pieces had to be removed. I was not aware that it's seemingly not common practice to have custom skeletons for the piercing and beard placements, many authors who have brilliantly sculpted heads also have floating piercings and gaping holes in their beards. Some inclusions might still be hit and miss in spots, but the large majority should have near perfect beard and piercing placements.
- Thanks to [UT - KAVT - Universal Automatic Patcher (UAP)](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/20629) patches and [Unique Tav Custom Appearance](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/2754) versions of head presets are no longer needed. To make the list more flexible I have removed all deprecated patches and swapped from UTCA versions of head presets to their base files.
- Dice were never really in the scope of DIQ Extras but I ended up dumping some in here, they've been moved to the main list because it's where they belong. [Dice Roulette](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/20857) makes more dice actually cool, and they're some very thematic and well made pieces from [elliegreenart](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/users/148115073) besides.
- The difficulty and ruleset files were overwritten in [Difficulty Immersion Quality Collection - Misc Files](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/9784) *DIQ Misc Patches - Overrides*, removing all difficulty selections except for Custom and Honour. If you're playing a campaign on a difficulty not recommend for DIQ, you might want to just not update I guess.
- No this isn't the 6 months or so overdue [Absolute's Laboratory - NPC and Combat Inspector and Mutator](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/16217) update, it'll come.
