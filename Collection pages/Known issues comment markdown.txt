# ❓Instructions for updating mods yourself

> **Note:**
>
> You can't just update everything some mod files are kept on older versions, or have had their version control done improperly leading to false positives. Others will need patches, or have dependencies and such. It's important that you only update things below, or bear the risk of going further. Generally however, if it's from me, [Cahooots](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/users/50497581), [osirisofinternet](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/users/6682532) or [actualsailorcat](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/users/78518828) it should be fine to update. To a lesser extent you should also feel comfortable updating mods from [DiZ91891](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/users/57776731) and [sumradagnoth8](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/users/186661377). I will flesh out a generalised list more in the future I guess.

1. Hit ***Check for Updates***, let it do its thing.\
   ![](https://i.postimg.cc/x1YFJZdH/image-2025-11-04-164633504.png)
2. Click the yellow/orange cloud icons next to the respective mods, in the ***Version*** column.\
   ![](https://i.postimg.cc/DZsqc2js/image-2025-11-04-164848893.png)
3. You can ignore the dependency warning when updating the mods, you won't be breaking anything.
4. ***Deploy*** mods in [Vortex](https://www.nexusmods.com/about/vortex/).
5. Most of the time you don't have to do anything in [BG3MM](https://github.com/LaughingLeader/BG3ModManager), I will specify below if you do.

<details><summary>⚡️Crashes, Freezes & Soft Locks</summary>
# ⚡️Crashes, Freezes & Soft Locks

### During gameplay - Engine limits:

- There is such thing as too much, if you play with an increased party size, piles of summons, lots of auras, your game is just going to be more crash prone, especially in Act III. I personally play with a party of 4 and don't have issues, a party of 6 is probably the reasonable maximum. If your machine/game can't handle all the craziness, try reducing it some. \
  \
  This will generally come back to being a memory/CPU bottleneck, but despite hardware the game has its own limits too, and the same can be said for general AI slowdown etc.

### On game launch:

- Make sure your system's anti-virus is not interfering with [Script Extender](https://github.com/Norbyte/bg3se), make an exception in your firewall(s) for BG3's .exe files if need be.
- Don't launch the game as ***Administrator***, with the exception of the initial launch on a fresh install where it asks for elevation to install redistributable etc.

### Starting a new game:

- There was a curious case where someone was crashing on attempting to launch a new game, it was due to their [RivaTuner Server Statistics](https://www.rivatuner.org) software running in the background and not being kept updated.\
  Make sure no 3rd party software can interfere, keep things nice and clean.
</details>

<details><summary>⚡️Cosmetic Issues</summary>
# ⚡️Cosmetic Issues

1. Some Origin characters have messed up colours of the character creation screen. This is a side effect of them being customisable, however it's **ONLY** on the character creation screen. They will not look like this as NPC party members.\
   \
   Having [Appearance Edit Enhanced](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/899) as an optional file is a headache for compatibility, as some mods need to be patched to work alongside it such as [Origin's Gods](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/6305), so it's included in the main list. It does not take very long at all to select the appropriate eye, hair and skin colours, and to stick a beard back on to Gale. If you're not playing as one of these 4 Origin characters, or weren't going to use their default appearances, then this is entirely irrelevant to you.\
   \
   Note: They may not look as cooked as they do below depending on other factors, such as optional mods that alter their default appearance somewhat fixing things.\
   \
   **WARNING:** Wyll is a special case when it comes to Origin character editing, if he undergoes a certain transformation in Act I it will likely remove customisations made to him. He will not be further editable without resetting his appearance to his human form, which can be done via [Script Extender](https://github.com/Norbyte/bg3se). Be aware, that this will break any race/body type changing you have done and remove his devil form permanently: `Osi.RemoveTransforms("S_Player_Wyll_c774d764-4a17-48dc-b470-32ace9ce447d")`\
   ![](https://i.postimg.cc/CKGsmgsf/origin-characters-at-home.png)
2. Selecting ***Randomise Appearance*** in character creation will likely lead to some less than desirable outcomes. For example, slots such as the Dragonborn Jaw, or Horns can be used for layering hairstyles if you wish (layering hairstyles etc. may cause physics to negatively interact with each other). There's a great number of options, and you're best to build your character piece by piece. \
   Unless you're looking for some inspiration, like I have below.\
   ![](https://i.postimg.cc/WbpYzXPP/Screenshot-2024-05-19-075923.png)
3. **Incorrect tooltip information**:\
   Tooltips often get stuck showing outdated information, because of this bug the information displayed may be incorrect. Save then load the game to refresh the UI if necessary.
4. Since ***Patch #7*** many custom icons have been cooked. Lots are patched, but a bunch of action resource icons might still be the purple star.
5. 🔞**In Game Nudity Toggles Not Working Properly:**\
   If a custom/modded item uses the chest/camp clothes slot and does not cover genitalia the game is generally assuming that it does. This circumvents the nudity being disabled. This may also be the case if a modded outfit does not have visuals for your race/gender.&#x20;
6. Unique bodies may not display correctly on clothing which modifies the body, such as outfits from [Scantily Camp Outfit](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/2617) and some from [Basket Full of Equipment. NSFW version](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/97).
7. NPCs when inspected will show as having 0m Movement Speed, they still move in combat so it's definitely just cosmetic, I have no idea why and should investigate one day.
8. [Ellian's heads](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/2939) has both Eyes of the Beholder and non-EOTB version of the heads in one download. Since we're not using EOTB, those additional heads will have mesh textured eyes and should not be used.
9. Selecting ***Randomise Appearance*** in character creation will likely lead to some less than desirable outcomes. E.g. physics negatively interacting with each other, nail options being body type specific etc.
