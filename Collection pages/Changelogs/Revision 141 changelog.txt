- Yeah I know, no [Absolute's Laboratory - NPC and Combat Inspector and Mutator](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/16217) again, but I didn't want to delay maintenance again again.\
  Before the new year is now the goal, and it's realistic enough.
- Changes from Revision 139-141 are not yet reflected on the [About](https://next.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/collections/pns4qv?tab=about) page.
- Issues with slow installations should be resolved.
- So turns out that the vanilla game actually handled items no longer being present when you removed mods and that is was a Script Extender bug that prevented normal procedure from taking place, resulting in games crashing etc. So from now on [Mod Uninstaller](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/9701) is going to be required a hell of a lot less, as this SE bug has been resolved at some point not too long ago. \
  TLDR; removing most mod from existing game don't crash game computer healthy good.
