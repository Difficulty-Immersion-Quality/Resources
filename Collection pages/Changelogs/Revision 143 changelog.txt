- There's one wee exception I suppose where it's heavily advised to respec your illithid powers. This can be done prior to updating by acquiring the Purity Tadpole from [Illithid Powers Overhaul 2](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/5105), easily acquired from Withers or spawning with Easy Cheat's item spawner in the mod configuration menu.

- If you don't use [DIQ Extras](https://www.nexusmods.com/games/baldursgate3/collections/k3zucg), then import the zip from [https://mod.io/g/baldursgate3/m/elledwyn-hairstyles1#description](https://mod.io/g/baldursgate3/m/elledwyn-hairstyles1#description "https://mod.io/g/baldursgate3/m/elledwyn-hairstyles1#description") into Vortex otherwise Kagha will be bald lmao.
