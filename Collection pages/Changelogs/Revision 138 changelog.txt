- Support for [DIQ Expanded](https://next.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/collections/y0iw7z) and [DIQ Armoury](https://next.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/collections/y7cdjf) is being dropped, select mods will be included in the main collection.

<details><summary>❓Why drop support for those 2 addon packs?</summary>
- When it comes to bugs and troubleshooting I have to rule out whether or not people use those as a variable each time, overall this complicates every process of elimination
- A number of bug reports are directly related to these mods, they tend to have more bugs and worse localisation
- I feel obligated to maintain quality, and that means patching things I don't really want to patch
- A lot of users assume more = better and download everything, then get a kitchen sink experience that does not match my vision at all
- There's constantly questions about poorly implemented crafting materials, and class specific items
- It's more mods in my workspace and game install, more mods that I otherwise would not be using that give me longer load times and VSC search times
- I have limited time, and have spent an absurd amount of it on DIQ, this would give me time back
- The userbase in the Discord will be playing a much similar game, instead of getting build recommendations for mods they don't have installed etc. causing a fractured social experience instead of everyone just playing DIQ as intended
- It's harder to gauge where difficulty should be for the list, when 50% of the userbase has a bunch of unintended OP crap, it robs me of good feedback
- People can just add a class mod or whatever and come to ⁠❗***bg3-modified-list-help*** because it's generally not rocket science to add them, with few exceptions
- Realistically the addon packs were a recycling bin for crap I no longer wanted, then just became a dumping ground for things others may find cool that didn't seem like they would set the list on fire; I have no passion for them at all.
</details>
