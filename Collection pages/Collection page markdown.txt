# ![](https://gitlab.com/Difficulty-Immersion-Quality/Difficulty-Immersion-Quality/-/wikis/uploads/Banners/Wiki_Banner_24.png)Welcome to Difficulty, Immersion, Quality!

DIQ is an obsessively curated, patched, and polished mod list both inspired by classic CRPGs and reasonably faithful to 5th Edition D\&D. The primary intention is to move BG3 away from being a theme park ride and introduce more gameplay elements one would expect of an immersive sim, elevating the already solid narrative driven RPG bones of the vanilla game.

DIQ has been built up over more than 2 years with thousands of hours spent on development and a firm intent to capture a specific vision, to make it ever more worthy of its name. Despite the core vision, DIQ also boasts a large amount of customisation options and even optional adult mods; no stone is left unturned in the pursuit of offering the most robust and attractive end user experience, so long as it doesn't conflict with the core vision of DIQ itself.

<details><summary>🐱Quality of Life: Cheating, Convenience, Performance & Time Savers</summary>
# 🐱Quality of Life: Cheating, Convenience, Performance & Time Savers

### Goated:

- [Smart Autosaving](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/5899) \
  Smart Autosaving is a mod designed to autosave regularly while minimizing gameplay interruption.\
  \
  **Condition-based autosaving**: The mod autosaves at regular intervals (10 minutes by default), but it also checks various in-game states, like whether the player is in dialogue, trading, combat, lockpicking, etc. Based on these checks, it decides whether to trigger an autosave.\
  \
  **Autosave postponement**: If a save is due (based on the timer) but the player is in a restricted state (like dialogue), instead of skipping the autosave entirely, the mod waits until the player leaves that state before autosaving.\
  \
  **Save and load-aware**: The mod will reset the timer if any other save is created, be it manual or otherwise, or when loading saves, so as to not create saves unnecessarily. You set it to save every 15 minutes but you saved at minute 14? It will now autosave only after minute 29.
- [Less Crowd Characters](https://www.nexusmods.com/baldursgate3/mods/9746) *None*.\
  Can help performance in Act III.
